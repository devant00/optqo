# DevAnt

I'm Anthony, founder and engineer behind **Optqo Framework s.r.o.** We build and operate two products with separate codebases. This page is a short public introduction; the details of how they run stay in their private repositories.

## Products

| Product | Private repository | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **GetTheCall / EmergencyLines** | `trades-platform` | Connects UK homeowners who need emergency trade work with local tradespeople. In production. |
| **Dispution** | `dispution` | Helps resolve disputes using policies and evidence. In development. |

GetTheCall is for tradespeople; EmergencyLines is for homeowners. Work on disputes in the trades platform helped shape Dispution, but it is a separate product.

The products have their own services and data. They share some engineering practices and internal operator tools, which live in `optqo-private`.

## What I work on

- Services for calls, messaging, payments, and customer journeys.
- Evidence and review workflows that support accountable decisions.
- Internal tools for operating and improving the products.

I handle product direction, engineering, and operations. AI coding agents help with implementation, testing, and documentation; I make the final decisions. :)
